# Design System: Vibrant Interlock

## Brand Personality

### Energetic and Playful

The high-contrast CMYK-inspired palette of cyan, magenta, and yellow creates a sense of vibrancy and creativity.

### Modern and Dynamic

The interlocking rings and long shadows suggest movement, connectivity, and a forward-thinking approach.

### Friendly and Accessible

Rounded forms and bright tones make the brand feel approachable and fun.

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

- Action Cyan: `#4DBEEE` - Used for primary actions, links, and accents.
- Electric Magenta: `#F04E98` - Used for secondary highlights and expressive elements.
- Vivid Yellow: `#F9F871` - Used sparingly for attention-grabbing accents or warnings.

### Neutral Colors

- Pure White: `#FFFFFF` - Primary background and surface color to let the brand colors pop.
- Deep Slate: `#2D3436` - Primary text color for high readability against white.
- Soft Gray: `#F5F6F7` - Secondary backgrounds and subtle borders.

## Typography

### Headings

Use a modern geometric sans-serif such as Montserrat or Poppins.

- Bold weights of `700+` for high impact.
- Tight, compact styling with letter spacing kept at `0`.

### Body

Use a clean sans-serif such as Inter or Roboto.

- Regular weight of `400` for readability.
- Generous line height around `1.6` for clarity.

## Visual Language

- Interlocking shapes: Use overlapping elements with subtle transparency or blend modes to mirror the logo structure.
- Long shadows: Apply 45-degree flat shadows to cards or buttons for a pop-art feel.
- Rounded corners: Use generous border radii from `12px` to `24px` to maintain the friendly circular theme.
- White space: Use generous spacing to balance the intense color palette.

## Component Patterns

- Buttons: Rounded buttons using Action Cyan backgrounds with white text.
- Cards: White cards with Soft Gray borders and Electric Magenta accent bars.
- Icons: Simple, thick-stroke icons using the brand colors.

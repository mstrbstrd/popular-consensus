# Decision register

Principles below capture the agreed direction. Mechanisms remain open until a
recorded decision states alternatives, rationale, invariant impact, migration,
review, and acceptance evidence. Do not treat an earlier recommendation as an
approved production selection.

## Accepted design direction

Privacy is first-class. People supply authority. Records describe expression over
time, not absolute truth. Social applications, plural community registries, and a
data union belong in the mission. Participation may earn compensation without
selling a preferred answer or surrendering unrelated privacy. Infrastructure must
be independently usable and communities must have practical exit/continuation.

## Decision record template

Record ID, status (Proposed/Accepted/Superseded), owner, scope, alternatives,
rationale, affected invariant IDs, unresolved risks, required reviews, migration,
conformance vectors, effective version, and links to acceptance evidence. Private
review material must have a safe disclosure policy; never publish private evidence
merely to complete a governance record.

## First-pilot selection

Select a consenting adult community, a concrete low-risk question, committed
funding, responsible facilitation, accessibility support, an independent evaluator,
and a named party responsible for responding. Cohort size and privacy thresholds
must follow the actual risk and study design. The pilot is not evidence that a
self-selected group represents humanity.
